# Sources, datasets and notices

The course material belongs to its respective authors. No blanket open-source
license is applied to all lecture text, photographs, figures or third-party code.
Preserve the source credits when using the teaching material.

- **OST branding and campus photographs:** supplied course assets. The lakefront
  cover was extracted unchanged from the lecturer's `AEVAE.pdf`; its checksum
  manifest is next to the JPEG in `lecture/brand/`.
- **Lecture illustrations:** visible image credits are retained. The instructor
  source keeps the original PDF; student packages contain the core HTML slides
  and their referenced illustrations, without the appendix or full source PDF.
  Frank Noé is credited on the applicable face illustrations.
- **Reveal.js 5.2.1:** MIT license in `lecture/assets/vendor/reveal/LICENSE`.
  The distribution also includes the notices bundled with its fonts and plugins.
- **Lato:** SIL Open Font License in `lecture/assets/vendor/lato/OFL.txt`.
- **Fashion-MNIST:** [Zalando Research](https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist),
  MIT notice in `lecture/assets/vendor/FASHION_MNIST_LICENSE.txt`.
- **Digits:** distributed with scikit-learn; see its
  [dataset documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_digits.html).
- **Foster:** teaching adaptations of *Generative Deep Learning*, Chapter 3,
  with links to the [official companion code](https://github.com/davidADSP/Generative_Deep_Learning_2nd_Edition/tree/main/notebooks/03_vae).
- **Raschka:** architecture adapted from STAT 453 lecture 17. The original MIT
  notice is in `lecture/instructor/raschka128/LICENSE_RASCHKA.txt` and the
  corresponding standalone notebook.
- **CelebA:** [original source and agreement](https://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/projects/CelebA.html).
  Download the aligned images and partition metadata separately. Its agreement
  limits use to non-commercial research and restricts redistribution of dataset
  photographs. The student model gallery includes generated outputs and decoded
  interpolations, with original CelebA test photographs omitted. The instructor's
  original galleries and data remain separate private materials.

The conceptual face illustrations in the lecture PDF are credited illustrations;
they are distinct from the original CelebA test photographs omitted from the
student model gallery. Static gallery response times are not inference benchmarks.
